# Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

**Conference Audio Recorder** by Siarhei Kuchuk

Application name: Conference Audio Recorder
Developer name: Siarhei Kuchuk

Conference Audio Recorder records the microphone and the speakers on this computer and saves MP3 files in a folder you choose. It does not create a developer account. The developer does not operate a backend that receives your recordings, settings, or usage data.

## Data the developer does not collect

The app does not include ads, analytics, crash reporters, or tracking. The developer does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

## Data stored on your computer

### Recordings

Audio you record is written only to the folder you choose. By default that folder is your Music folder, in a subfolder named in the language of the app. Those files are not uploaded to the developer.

### Settings

The chosen folder, selected devices, theme, and the language last used for the license, privacy policy, and third-party notices are stored only on this computer:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\ConferenceAudioRecorder\1.0\Settings`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Settings`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Settings`

### Logs

Diagnostic logs are written only on this computer:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\ConferenceAudioRecorder\1.0\Logs`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Logs`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Logs`

Those files are not sent anywhere.

## Network use

The app does not upload recordings and does not contact the developer. It can open these pages in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies:

- [Project homepage](https://github.com/drweb86/conference-audio-recorder)
- [Questions and bugs](https://github.com/drweb86/conference-audio-recorder/issues)

The license, this privacy policy, and the third-party notices are shown inside the app. Installs from the Microsoft Store are delivered by Microsoft. The app itself does not send usage data to the Store.

## Children

The app records audio on this computer. It is not directed at children under 13.

## Third parties

GitHub processes the pages you open. The Microsoft Store processes Store installs and updates. The developer does not receive that traffic.

## Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted in this file in the project repository.
