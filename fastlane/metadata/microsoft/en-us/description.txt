Conference Audio Recorder records the microphone and the speakers during a conference call, including Microsoft Teams, Skype, and Viber. It saves an MP3 of the mixed audio and keeps the microphone and speaker files next to it.

Choose the folder, the sample rate, and the MP3 quality. The app opens in a dark theme. You can switch to a light theme or follow the system theme.

Recordings stay on this PC. The app does not upload them, and it has no ads and no tracking. There is no account.

Conference Audio Recorder is open source. Its own code is CC0 1.0 Universal. Third-party components keep their own licenses.
