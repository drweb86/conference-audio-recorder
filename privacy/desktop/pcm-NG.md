# Privacy policy

Last update: 26 September 2026

**Conference Audio Recorder** by Siarhei Kuchuk

App name: Conference Audio Recorder
Developer: Siarhei Kuchuk

Conference Audio Recorder dey record microphone and speaker for this computer, and e dey save MP3 file for the folder wey you choose. E no dey create developer account. The developer no get server wey dey receive your recordings, settings, or how you dey use the app.

## Data wey the developer no dey collect

The app no get advert, analytics, crash report, or tracking. The developer no dey collect, sell, or share personal data.

## Data wey dey this computer

### Recordings

The audio wey you record dey write only for the folder wey you choose. By default na your Music folder, with subfolder for the language of the app. Those files no dey go the developer.

### Settings

The folder wey you choose, devices, theme, and the language wey you last use for license, privacy, and third-party notices dey stay only for this computer:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\ConferenceAudioRecorder\1.0\Settings`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Settings`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Settings`

### Logs

Diagnostic logs dey write only for this computer:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\ConferenceAudioRecorder\1.0\Logs`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Logs`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Logs`

Those files no dey go anywhere.

## Network

The app no dey upload recordings and e no dey contact the developer. E fit open these pages for browser. Those sites get their own privacy policy:

- [Project page](https://github.com/drweb86/conference-audio-recorder)
- [Questions and bugs](https://github.com/drweb86/conference-audio-recorder/issues)

The license, this privacy policy, and third-party notices dey show inside the app. Microsoft Store installs na Microsoft dey deliver them. The app itself no dey send usage data to the Store.

## Children

The app dey record audio for this computer. E no be for children wey never reach 13 years.

## Third parties

GitHub dey process the pages wey you open. Microsoft Store dey process Store installs and updates. The developer no dey receive that traffic.

## Changes

Updates to this policy go dey for this file for the project repository.
