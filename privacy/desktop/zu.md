# Inqubomgomo yobumfihlo

Igcinwe ukubuyekezwa: 26 September 2026

**Conference Audio Recorder** by Siarhei Kuchuk

Igama lohlelo: Conference Audio Recorder
Umthuthukisi: Siarhei Kuchuk

I-Conference Audio Recorder irekhoda imakrofoni nezikhulumi kule khompyutha bese igcina amafayela e-MP3 kufolda oyikhethayo. Ayidali i-akhawunti yomthuthukisi. Umthuthukisi akanawo iseva elamukela okuqoshiwe, izilungiselelo, noma idatha yokusetshenziswa.

## Idatha umthuthukisi angayiqoqi

Uhlelo alunazo izikhangiso, ukuhlaziya, imibiko yokuphahlazeka, noma ukulandelela. Umthuthukisi akaqoqi, athengise, noma abelane ngedatha yomuntu.

## Idatha kule khompyutha

### Okuqoshiwe

Umsindo oqoshiwe ubhalwa kufolda oyikhethayo kuphela. Ngokuzenzakalelayo kufolda Yomculo, enefolda encane ngolimi lohlelo. Lawa mafayela awathunyelwa kumthuthukisi.

### Izilungiselelo

Ifolda ekhethiwe, amadivayisi, itimu, nolimi olusetshenziswe okokugcina kwelayisensi, ubumfihlo, nezaziso zabantu besithathu kugcinwa kule khompyutha kuphela:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\ConferenceAudioRecorder\1.0\Settings`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Settings`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Settings`

### Amarekhodi

Amarekhodi okuxilonga abhalwa kule khompyutha kuphela:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\ConferenceAudioRecorder\1.0\Logs`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Logs`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Logs`

Lawa mafayela awathunyelwa ndawo.

## Inethiwekhi

Uhlelo alulayishi okuqoshiwe futhi aluxhumani nomthuthukisi. Lingavula lezi zikhasi kusiphequluli. Lezo zindawo zinenqubomgomo yazo:

- [Ikhasi lomsebenzi](https://github.com/drweb86/conference-audio-recorder)

Ilayisensi, le nqubomgomo, nezaziso zabantu besithathu kuboniswa ngaphakathi kohlelo. Ukufakwa okuvela ku-Microsoft Store kuhlinzekwa ngu-Microsoft. Uhlelo uqobo aluthumeli idatha yokusetshenziswa ku-Store.
