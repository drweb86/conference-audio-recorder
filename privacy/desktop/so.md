# Siyaasadda asturnaanta

Cusboonaysiintii ugu dambeysay: 26 September 2026

**Conference Audio Recorder** by Siarhei Kuchuk

Magaca abka: Conference Audio Recorder
Horumariyaha: Siarhei Kuchuk

Conference Audio Recorder wuxuu duubaa makarafoonka iyo sameecadaha kumbuyuutarkan wuxuuna kaydiyaa faylasha MP3 galka aad doorato. Ma abuuro akoon horumariye. Horumariyuhu ma maamulo server qaata duubistaada, dejinta, ama xogta isticmaalka.

## Xogta horumariyuhu aanu ururin

Abku ma laha xayeysiis, falanqayn, warbixin burbur, ama dabaggal. Horumariyuhu ma ururiyo, ma iibiyo, mana wadaago xogta shaqsiga.

## Xogta ku jirta kumbuyuutarkan

### Duubista

Codka la duubay waxaa lagu qoraa kaliya galka aad doorato. Caadi ahaan waa galka Muusiga, oo leh galka hoose ee luqadda abka. Faylashaas looma diro horumariyaha.

### Dejinta

Galka la doortay, aaladaha, mawduuca, iyo luqadda ugu dambeysay ee loo isticmaalay shatiga, asturnaanta, iyo ogeysiisyada dhinaca saddexaad waxaa lagu hayaa kumbuyuutarkan oo keliya:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\ConferenceAudioRecorder\1.0\Settings`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Settings`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Settings`

### Diiwaannada

Diiwaannada baaritaanka waxaa lagu qoraa kumbuyuutarkan oo keliya:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\ConferenceAudioRecorder\1.0\Logs`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Logs`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Logs`

Faylashaas meelna looma diro.

## Shabakad
