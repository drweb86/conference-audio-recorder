# Manufar sirri

An sabunta karshe: 26 September 2026

**Conference Audio Recorder** by Siarhei Kuchuk

Sunan app: Conference Audio Recorder
Mai haɓakawa: Siarhei Kuchuk

Conference Audio Recorder yana rikodin makirufo da lasifika a wannan kwamfuta kuma yana ajiye fayilolin MP3 a foldar da ka zaɓa. Ba ya ƙirƙirar asusun mai haɓakawa. Mai haɓakawa ba ya gudanar da uwar garke da ke karɓar rikodi, saituna, ko bayanan amfani.

## Bayanan da mai haɓakawa ba ya tattarawa

App ba ta da talla, nazari, rahoton rushewa, ko bin diddigi. Mai haɓakawa ba ya tattarawa, sayarwa, ko raba bayanan sirri.

## Bayanan da ke wannan kwamfuta

### Rikodi

Sautin da aka rikodi ana rubuta shi ne kawai a foldar da ka zaɓa. Ta tsohuwa ita ce foldar Kiɗa, tare da ƙaramin folda a yaren app. Ba a tura waɗannan fayiloli ga mai haɓakawa.

### Saituna

Zaɓaɓɓen folda, na'urori, jigo, da yaren da aka yi amfani da shi na ƙarshe don lasisi, sirri, da sanarwar wasu na uku ana ajiye su a wannan kwamfuta kawai:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\ConferenceAudioRecorder\1.0\Settings`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Settings`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Settings`

### Rajista

Ana rubuta rajistan bincike a wannan kwamfuta kawai:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\ConferenceAudioRecorder\1.0\Logs`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Logs`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Logs`

Ba a tura waɗannan fayiloli ko'ina.

## Hanyar sadarwa

App ba ta loda rikodi kuma ba ta tuntuɓar mai haɓakawa. Za ta iya buɗe waɗannan shafuka a burauza. Waɗannan shafuka suna da nasu manufofi:

- [Shafin aikin](https://github.com/drweb86/conference-audio-recorder)
- [Tambayoyi da kurakurai](https://github.com/drweb86/conference-audio-recorder/issues)

Ana nuna lasisi, wannan manufar, da sanarwar wasu na uku a cikin app. Microsoft ke bayar da shigarwa daga Microsoft Store. App kanta ba ta aika bayanan amfani zuwa Store.

## Yara

App tana rikodin sauti a wannan kwamfuta. Ba ta da niyya ga yara ƙasa da shekara 13.

## Wasu na uku

GitHub yana sarrafa shafukan da kake buɗewa. Microsoft Store yana sarrafa shigarwa da sabuntawa na Store. Mai haɓakawa ba ya karɓar wannan zirga-zirga.

## Canje-canje
