# Amụma nzuzo

Emelitere ikpeazụ: 26 September 2026

**Conference Audio Recorder** by Siarhei Kuchuk

Aha ngwa: Conference Audio Recorder
Onye mepụtara: Siarhei Kuchuk

Conference Audio Recorder na-edekọ maịk na okwu n'ịntanet kọmputa a ma chekwaa faịlụ MP3 n'ime folda ị họọrọ. Ọ naghị emepụta akaụntụ onye mepụtara. Onye mepụtara anaghị arụ sava nke na-anata ndekọ gị, ntọala, ma ọ bụ data ojiji.

## Data onye mepụtara anaghị anakọta

Ngwa enweghị mgbasa ozi, nyocha, akụkọ ndakpọ, ma ọ bụ nsochi. Onye mepụtara anaghị anakọta, ere, ma ọ bụ kesaa data nkeonwe.

## Data dị na kọmputa a

### Ndekọ

A na-ede ụda e dekọrọ naanị n'ime folda ị họọrọ. Nke ndabara bụ folda Egwu, nwere folda nta n'asụsụ ngwa. A naghị eziga faịlụ ndị a nye onye mepụtara.

### Ntọala

Folda a họọrọ, ngwaọrụ, isiokwu, na asụsụ e ji ikpeazụ maka ikike, nzuzo, na ọkwa ndị ọzọ ka a na-echekwa naanị na kọmputa a:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\ConferenceAudioRecorder\1.0\Settings`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Settings`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Settings`

### Lọọgụ

A na-ede lọọgụ nyocha naanị na kọmputa a:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\ConferenceAudioRecorder\1.0\Logs`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Logs`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/ConferenceAudioRecorder/1.0/Logs`

A naghị eziga faịlụ ndị ahụ ebe ọ bụla.

## Netwọk

Ngwa anaghị ebugo ndekọ ma ọ naghị akpọtụrụ onye mepụtara. Ọ nwere ike imepe ibe ndị a na ihe nchọgharị. Ebe ndị ahụ nwere amụma nke ha:

- [Ibe oru](https://github.com/drweb86/conference-audio-recorder)
- [Ajụjụ na njehie](https://github.com/drweb86/conference-audio-recorder/issues)

A na-egosi ikike, amụma a, na ọkwa ndị ọzọ n'ime ngwa. Microsoft na-enye ntinye sitere na Microsoft Store. Ngwa n'onwe ya anaghị eziga data ojiji na Store.

## Ụmụaka

Ngwa na-edekọ ụda na kọmputa a. Ọ bụghị maka ụmụaka ndị na-erubeghị afọ 13.

## Ndị ọzọ

GitHub na-ahazi ibe ị mepere. Microsoft Store na-ahazi ntinye na mmelite Store. Onye mepụtara anaghị enweta okporo ụzọ ahụ.

## Mgbanwe

A ga-ebipụta mmelite amụma a na faịlụ a n'ụlọ nchekwa oru.
